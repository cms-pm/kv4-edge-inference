C. VRAM Scheduler
The VRAM scheduler is the core contribution of LegacyRAG.
Before every embedding operation it runs:
nvidia-smi --query-gpu=index,memory.free
--format=csv,noheader
and routes by the following policy:
1) If any GPU reports free VRAM below Tvram (default:
800 MB), route to CPU.
2) If a GenerationContext lock is active (generation
in progress), route to CPU regardless of VRAM state.
3) Otherwise, route to GPU.
Every decision is timestamped and appended
